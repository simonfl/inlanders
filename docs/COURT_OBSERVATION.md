# Willow court — arrangement and ordinary life comparison

Compare `./Review.ps1 Inspect neighborhood-inherited` with `./Review.ps1 Inspect willow-court`. For growth use `willow-court-expanded`; it has sixteen residents after real construction and invitation. Use 960/1440 and the opposite view (`-Turn 2`). These are combined experience alternatives, not an isolated art or balance A/B: home/venue placement, paths, tree placement, framing and interaction differ. Initial people, building kinds and food/timber quantities match.

Give this invitation without a solution: **Make an everyday place you like. You can keep what works, change the arrangement, or welcome more people when you choose.** Do not prescribe a garden, bridge or home move. Pause for decisions; use 3× for production waits and 1× to judge movement and sound.

Record before acting:

- What appears to be happening? Where are homes, meals and gathering space?
- What would you change, and what do you expect residents to do differently?
- Screenshot/F8 bundle, commit/build, selected resident, pause/speed and any open panel.

Record after the intervention and after the resident's next meal:

- Actual action, time waiting, any help or misunderstanding.
- What changed visibly? Did it match the prediction?
- Was a mistake recoverable? Did the trial restore work or explain a blocked return?
- Stop, welcome newcomers, keep arranging or watch? Why?
- Preferred version and what should be removed.

The blue line is an available route **now**, not a reserved/predicted next meal. Gold follows a claimed meal's actual remaining route. The card names current activity or the immediate shortage/access problem. A trial relocates one building; restoring returns its position/orientation, not elapsed time, consumed food, jobs or removed paths. Keep its old footprint clear if you want to restore immediately. Building elsewhere and no-change are valid alternatives.

Falsify the candidate if people need source/coaching to diagnose it, every answer is another garden, changes are visible only in statistics, the composition works from only one camera, or they still see required construction followed by waiting. Record unavailable human observation/audio honestly. Scripted checks do not fill in these answers.
