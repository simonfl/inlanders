## Verdict and direction

**Partially convincing as a village arrangement prototype; not yet a convincing sustained campaign.** The strongest prospective pleasure is making useful places fit together and seeing residents inhabit them. Actual journeys, construction and recovery make that more than decorative placement. We have not established how often players recognize a consequential choice, enjoy making it, or want to keep watching afterward.

Willow is delivered as a playable comparison, not accepted as a deeper second level. Keep its finite welcome as an optional stopping point. The next experiment changes the central activity: choose an everyday place or household, understand a journey, reshape that place, and recognize the resulting life. Combine F30a world interaction and F30b lane/court composition into one coherent slice. Shipping another overlay and deferring the visual reward would shrink the structural finding into an incremental UI task.

Freeze additional levels, resources and needs. Opening and inlet remain the primary comparative material; meadow is labelled a comparison prototype. Retain legacy maps and tests as development assets, but do not restore their ten-level assessment sequence as the product spine. This is a decision to test a different experience, not a claim that endless play is better or that users prefer repairing inherited work.

## Evidence that changes the plan

The [five inlet arms](INHERITED_SHORELINE_F29C.md) use one common start and real construction. Footway, bridge and local-food plans complete at approximately 422 seconds. Bridge/local food reduce measured food walking about 9%/7%, but those effects have no demonstrated perceptual payoff. The eastern-home plan completes at 516 seconds, then accumulates **612.4 hungry resident-seconds** in a 600-second observation. Lower walking in that arm is not an efficiency win. A western garden later reaches zero hungry time after 300 seconds settling and a further 600-second window. The recovery is real in that continuation; its cause is not isolated without a same-age unchanged control.

The completion predicate checks current nourishment and reachability to an open stocked producer. One available portion can satisfy reachability for several people. It is a welcome endpoint, not evidence of continuing production or one available meal per resident. The reviewed UI overclaims: “This settlement is complete” and “Nothing else is required.” Correct the contract; do not introduce another stockpile target or rolling certification window.

The fixed 960 inlet opening explains the banks but covers western homes with Goals; invitation is below the fold. At 1440, simultaneous Goals and inspector still occupy much of the village. The food view exposes free/claimed stock and real routes, but does not explain why one household's arrangement fails. More reservation-policy exposition is not the answer.

Fresh default dense imagery shows recognizable bakery/chimney and civic masses, but crowded roofs, tiny floating labels and little spatial hierarchy. The title scene makes a stronger compositional promise than ordinary play. Warm roofs and useful silhouettes deserve retention; wholesale model replacement is not yet justified. Default ground, connected public space, landscape enclosure, camera and label hierarchy need a composed comparison at ordinary zoom, including a dense extension and opposite views.

## Retain, cut and reconsider across the game

- **Retain provisionally:** shared available-job allocation, physical meal collection, visible construction, unrestricted homes/bridges and manual invitation. They support understandable intentions and spatial consequences. Optional dedicated workers remain an advanced intervention, not the initial lesson.
- **Food and materials:** retain geographically distinct foraging, gardens, fishing and hunting as experimental choices. Farm/bakery processing, storage and timber/plank housing can change movement and investment. Orchard, narrow stone demand and comfort must earn their experiential role; availability is not a reason to require them.
- **Civic buildings/needs:** treat garden, square and hall as candidate expressive scales of a communal place. Different capacity/memory values alone do not justify separate objectives. Preserve eating, home return and recreation as visible routines; stop expanding happiness arithmetic, diet quotas and rolling recency requirements.
- **Campaign:** salvage river access, limited lake shore/replenishing fish, near/far quarry, renewable woodland and crowded-center geography. Consolidate or archive introductory recipe levels. Do not preserve assessment machinery because its tests pass.
- **Creative:** retain free construction and forgiving hunger intent. Next unify it with the selected village work/food rules so residents still collect meals and inhabit the arrangement. Current alternate role model is a real learning and maintenance cost.
- **Controls/presentation:** replace panel-led diagnosis with direct household/place interaction. Test normal-play reversibility so expensive demolition does not make the comparison meaningful only in Creative. Choose a default composition direction, not another permanent study toggle.
- **Architecture/reliability:** keep Godot/C#, current-format save discipline and existing capture infrastructure. No engine/ECS rewrite, migrations or generic mode framework. Once the experience chooses supported rules, retire unsuccessful ordinary entry paths and reduce the actual mode combinations.

## Agreements, disagreements and lead choice

All roles question adding content before making spatial consequences understandable. The designer favors expressive composition; lead/playtest emphasize diagnosing a daily inconvenience; UX requests household preview/reversibility. These are not identical proposals. A compulsory hunger crisis could turn a peaceful composition game into maintenance, while pure decoration could erase the logistics that make changes matter.

Choose a paired **arrangement-and-life** experiment: current inlet control and an inhabited lane/court candidate with the same population, production capability and initial resources. Keep no-change, imperfect-layout and add-elsewhere controls. Welcome is optional in the candidate. Show actual journeys, allow a reversible arrangement opportunity, then observe ordinary life. Do not promise projected efficiency until simulation data supports the prediction. Use the preserved eastern-home case for diagnosis and recovery, but do not manufacture a compulsory shortage or demolition objective.

Observe intended change, predicted consequence, action, next meal, confusion, idle time, recovery and voluntary continuation. Fail if changes are invisible, every solution is another garden, diagnosis requires developer explanation, or a route score replaces the old checklist. If arranging is enjoyable but logistics are not, choose a forgiving composition game explicitly. If the finite welcome provides needed purpose, retain it. Human preference remains unresolved; build the alternatives rather than claim acceptance.
