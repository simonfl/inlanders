# Whole-game review after F29b

September 13, 2026. Requested by the user at **19 playable outcomes**, after the [meadow decision experiment](MEADOW_DECISION_F29B.md). Five fresh independent read-only reviewer contexts: game design, UX/onboarding, playtest, development lead, visual/audio. This restores the independence missing at review fifteen. The experiment, review and recovery-control cleanup do not add a playable outcome. The regular review at **20** remains due after the next complete playable outcome.

## Verdict and decision

**Partially convincing: a promising physical village simulation, with an underdeveloped reason to make the next village.** All five reviewers reached this verdict independently. The clearest current pleasure is placing useful buildings and seeing people construct, carry, cross water and eat. That is a positive case for retaining the simulation. It does not establish that the campaign creates worthwhile decisions, that ordinary life is enjoyable to watch, or that the catalogue's breadth improves the experience.

Keep the finite welcome as a provisional primary arc. **Replace the next second-settlement bet with an inherited shoreline village that the player reshapes before welcoming newcomers.** The existing meadow remains a comparison control, not the template for more levels. This is a change to the starting situation and the player's central action—revising a working place—rather than another food threshold, prescribed chain or compulsory land sacrifice. F29c is the next playable slice.

Do not switch to an endless transformation game merely because source reviewers can imagine it. Review fifteen proposed recurring transformation; F28c could not justify that as the central reward. This review supplies no human preference evidence that overturns that limit. Test transformation **inside a finite situation**, with optional continued play. Compare doing nothing and simply adding elsewhere, and accept compact bypasses. If those are better experiences, the scenario must change; it must not ban them to protect its premise.

## What the experiment establishes

Eight informed meadow plans vary food chain, home layout and invitation timing from one saved start. All finish without hunger during preparation; all exceed 32 stored portions before welcome completion. Early invitation is faster in four of four matched pairs. Garden packages cost 52 logs; bread packages 64. Results are not identical—bread with early arrivals and split homes is fastest—but no tested plan demonstrates a consequential invitation risk or a reserve wait. See [all results and limits](MEADOW_DECISION_F29B.md).

The playtest reviewer supplies crucial counterevidence: the homes-first rendered route reaches 16/16 welcome and 8/8 newcomer housing with only 2/32 food and hunger at 571.51 simulation seconds. Recovery finishes at 1110.58s. **The reserve prevents a real premature win.** Reject reserve accumulation as the future scenario's identity, not its demonstrated safeguard in this old control. Do not remove it alone and declare a better level.

These are authored recipes with sequential construction and automatic legal-site selection. They establish feasibility and consequences, not a complete strategy space, human skill, discovery or enjoyment. Prepared completion spans roughly four to six minutes at 3x before player decision time. The user's concern about short, tutorial-like content remains unresolved.

## Whole-project keep, cut and redesign

**Campaign and core loop.** Keep one guided opening and finite completion while testing the inherited village. Stop expanding the ten legacy levels' building recipes, recent-service percentages and delivery certificates. Preserve river/lake/quarry/woods geography as reusable authored material. The same map with fewer berries and more arrivals does not establish campaign variety. More waiting is not the requested greater depth.

**Economy, costs and buildings.** Retain the four-log garden versus four-log farm/eight-log bakery; they differ in startup, processing and travel. Keep six-log/two-bed cottages versus twelve-plank/four-bed lodges as an available investment choice. Four-log stockpiles and six-log pantries have situational logistical value; do not prescribe them universally. Fish, wildlife, orchard maturity and woodland recovery deserve geography-led use, not mandatory collection. Quarry and the costly hall should not require visitation quotas to defeat cheaper useful venues. The seating garden, square and hall have different capacity/cadence, but distinct player value remains uncertain. Do not add another venue or producer to avoid deciding this.

**Needs.** Meals, homes and rest produce understandable daily activity. Recreation and optional comfort can support expression, but have not earned more targets or penalties. No new education/religion/need meters in the next slice. All existing buildings remain available; availability does not mean each deserves promotion into an objective.

**Controls and onboarding.** Retain the contextual inspector and direct links to real destinations. Replace confusing shared-work advice as part of the new slice: automatic workers should not be told to adopt legacy roles merely because their current job differs. Test selecting a struggling resident/source, seeing the cause and changing the world without hunting through several panels. “Stay and reshape” currently enters Watch; separate watching from building. Avoid solving either issue by adding more paragraphs. Removal under Details is not proven undiscoverable by a scripted click failure; test actual discovery separately.

**Normal and Creative.** Creative currently removes meal consumption and teaches older central-food/role rules. It is useful for arrangement but not a relaxed version of the most promising daily-life simulation. After choosing the slice, prototype free construction and forgiving hunger under the same local-food/shared-work rules, while people still eat. Retire obsolete player-facing rule branches only when their useful capabilities are covered; no save migrations or generalized mode framework.

**Visual direction.** Keep the existing material language. Test buildings addressing a lane or court, connected worn ground, a visible communal focus and vegetation enclosing rather than concealing work. Compare opposite viewing directions and dense expansion. The image must remain playable and freely rearrangeable, not become a fixed title-screen tableau. Current [dense study](review19-dense-study.png), [commons study](review19-commons-study.png) and [menu](review19-menu.png) show the scale of this problem. Study renders enable the optional Storybook comparison; they are **not the universal default**. Default journey captures retain older board/labels. The presentation direction still needs an explicit selection rather than accumulating more study toggles.

**Audio and motion.** Existing spatial voices, cooldowns, music themes and quiet intervals justify audition, not more content. A 20-second normal-speed movie was captured and its media integrity checked. No reviewer continuously watched or listened, and it is too short to judge musical repetition. Audio quality, animation contact and willingness to watch remain open. Do not claim acceptance from frame or cue counts.

**Reliability and performance.** Both fresh primary journeys and exact save/load checks pass. Atomic contention tests pass. The broad legacy menu smoke exposed stale hidden-control navigation and a obsolete backup assumption; cleanup is recorded below. Food-overlay reads while hidden, synchronous save retries and real-time pacing warrant a measured profile, not speculative optimization. Concurrent capture wall times are not gameplay FPS.

## Alternatives, disagreements and chosen experiment

1. **Keep the present meadow and tune quotas:** rejected as the next direction. It distinguishes inadequate provisioning but has not demonstrated a richer decision. Raising the reserve would manufacture waiting.
2. **Endless expressive transformation:** designer's legitimate alternative, but weaker endpoint and no new voluntary-play evidence. Defer as the primary game; include optional continuation as an observation.
3. **Inherited village, finite welcome:** selected comparison. Begin with functioning homes/supply but an understandable awkward relationship between food, homes and useful ground. Allow investing in access, bringing production closer, adding homes elsewhere, leaving existing work intact, or making a different layout. Use actual stock and routes. Do not require demolition, a rectangle, a specified venue, a service window or a speed score.
4. **Whole art replacement or architecture rewrite:** rejected now. There is positive evidence for the palette and physical simulation; test scene composition and supported rules before replacing them.

The playtest reviewer would retain the reserve provisionally; design/UX/lead would cut it as the scenario premise. The synthesis retains the safeguard in the frozen control and removes it from the proposed **already viable** alternative's defining objective. A welcome-only ending must still be challenged with an underprovisioned counterexample; success is not assumed because the initial village works.

F29c must expose at least two plausible interventions and retain no-change/add-elsewhere controls. Record what a player predicts, changes and notices, rather than inventing a compulsory efficiency test. Reject or reshape the prototype if people simply repeat the old recipe beside the inherited buildings, cannot see a consequence, or find it unwanted cleanup. Test a mistake and recovery with equal observation windows. Native UI control is unavailable here; uncoached human observation is outstanding, and more scripted successes cannot settle that question.

## Tooling decisions

**Accept alongside F29c:** extend the existing launcher with the paired state and a short observer record: first intended change, explanation, confusion, intervention, idle stretch and preferred outcome. Add step timestamps/awaited conditions to existing probes when touching them. Beneficiaries: every design/UX/recovery comparison. Estimated hours to half a day, low maintenance; measure time to launch, locate the first misunderstanding and explain a failed arm. Do not build a second runner or automatic “fun score.” Actual observation, not bundle generation, is the success criterion.

**Accept bounded measurement after the slice:** ordinary/dense 1x/6x, food overlay off/on/hidden, camera movement and saves using existing traces. Estimated several hours; avoids speculative performance edits. Optimize only attributed costs. T04 already measured useful fixture savings; no new dependency graph is justified.

**Reuse, don't expand:** current movie/audio capture. A quiet/busy/meal audition with somebody actually watching/listening has more value than another format pipeline. Measure time to start an audition and record whether it was consumed. Native-control or human access is an evidence dependency, not something a generic replay framework fixes.

Defer ECS, generic content editor, universal configuration, asset migration, additional sound themes and save compatibility. Next work is one coherent playable comparison, followed by the still-required checkpoint-20 review; [the queue](NEXT_CHUNKS.md) keeps subsequent work conditional.
