## What is playable

One village develops through three announced chapters: establish a livelihood for four, house/feed four newcomers, then choose and send a contribution to another settlement upriver. The player retains the same buildings, routes, woodland and stock throughout. Gardens, larger fields, fishing, homes, renewable groves, clearing intentions, an optional shortcut and same-ground garden entrance changes remain available. Preparing early is valid; no later loss invalidates it.

Start with two homes, four people, twelve provisions and ten logs. Establishment requires four local food deliveries and one founder's fresh meal. Newcomers arrive after480 simulated seconds or can be welcomed with eight beds and twelve food. Once all eight are housed and fed, choose64logs/24food or24logs/64food. Cargo consumes actual unclaimed central supplies, excludes opening provisions, protects existing claims, and packs food only above two available portions per resident. Normal eating can lower the remaining reserve afterward. Cancellation returns unfinished cargo; sending moves it once into exported accounting.

Dispatch requires full cargo, eight housed/fed people and sixteen remaining food. The player explicitly sends the boat, watches its twelve-second1×departure, and receives an actual contribution summary. Prior speed is restored at the paused ending. Continued tending remains available. No mandatory sustained-service window, new need, production tier, inheritance or season was added.
