# Whole-game review60 — choose a place worth changing

## Verdict and decision

**Partially convincing as a finite village transformation; sustained settlement play remains unproven.** Physical meals, shared work, recoverable rearrangement and an inhabited start give changes consequences. Five more implemented features have made arrangement easier to understand and perform. They have not established why a player personally wants the second change, or a third.

Choose finite, self-directed transformations as the working product hypothesis now. Consolidate the public experience around one retained inhabited place and compare a more convincing functional working landscape against the compact hamlet. Stop extending experimental branches and diagnostic UI as a substitute for choosing a product. This is a reversible design decision, not acceptance of fun or a declaration that the requested deeper campaign exists.

The strongest counterargument is important: kitchen gardens by homes already express the promised agrarian life. Clearing them for common ground is not inherently better. Preserve that disagreement. A player who prefers the gardens may have understood the game perfectly. A personally satisfactory ending should not certify a superior layout.

## Whole-game disposition

- **Campaign:** existing geographic dilemmas remain useful; historical certificates and a ten-level ladder are not commitments. The public farmstead and court still exist at this freeze. F38 proposes consolidation; it has not shipped in these five chunks.
- **Economy/buildings/needs:** retain physical goods, cultivation, shared labor, home rest, meals, recreation and recoverable commitment. Eighteen available buildings do not imply eighteen distinct valuable decisions. Audit overlapping social buildings before adding needs or a milling chain.
- **Normal/relaxed:** use the same retained place and daily life, with different constraints. Older public Free/court is not proof of acceptance of the proposed same-place relationship.
- **Controls/onboarding:** garden movement, staging, editable paths and direct inspection support experimentation. A/B routes explain current access, not hypothetical production. Panels must not displace watching and acting in the world.
- **Presentation/audio:** crops, cargo, tools and useful paths deserve retention because they reflect actual work. Broad generic ground, repeated homes and small detached garden tokens weaken the theme. No new household economy or inheritance is implied by grouping homes/land. Audio source has bounded voices/cooldowns and quiet music intervals; this is not listening evidence.
- **Reliability/performance:** current saves matter despite disposability. Intermittent replacement denial remains unresolved. A dense imported snapshot is diagnostic coverage, not natural-growth or representative performance acceptance.
