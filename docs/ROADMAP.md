## The game we are building toward

**Make a home by the river, make room for others, and make the resulting village worth staying in.** One authored settlement should develop through connected decisions about land, livelihood and useful improvements. People handle ordinary work. The player chooses what to commit to, sees the consequence and shapes the same place over time.

Aim for a substantial first story of roughly 20–30 minutes, without enforced waiting. Begin with food concern, develop an established village, and end with people visibly living and eating in the place the player made. Good preparation should pay off. Later situations must not arbitrarily invalidate an efficient beginning. Continued tending remains available after success.

New France is expressed through the inhabited farmstead, river frontage, cultivated ground, retained woodland, practical timber buildings and daily work. Descendants are narrative only. Seasons and inheritance remain excluded.

## Integrated requirements

**Purpose and choice.** Keep clear progress, mild recoverable food pressure and useful automatic work. Trade, food types and buildings earn a place through different land, timing or access consequences. No more arrival waves solely to enlarge the counters. No mandatory producer recipes, diet ratios, recent-service certificates or worker assignment panel.

**A village worth observing.** Preserve a substantial phone viewport as commitments accumulate. Compact persistent purpose/progress; detailed explanations on inspection. Compose homes, fields, woodlot and working routes as one inhabited place. Show clearing, delivered materials, construction, occupation and actual journeys. A small auditioned work/river sound layer belongs with the slice. No separate decoration backlog or large soundtrack project.

**Scope.** Timber, food, homes, grove, gardens/fields, fishing and access are enough for the next test. Native storage, processing chains, comfort, civic needs and Creative are references, not a porting checklist. Keep native modes available without maintaining browser parity. No new needs or production tier until the existing small game gives a convincing reason to return.

**Delivery.** Keep the browser client and shared C# simulation on Windows, private phone access, ordinary player/agent commands and recorded sessions. Rewrite or move hosting only when a measured bottleneck warrants it. Saves remain disposable across formats; current saves must work.
