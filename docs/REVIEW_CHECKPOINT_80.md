## Verdict and direction

**Partially convincing as a small inhabited farmstead; motivation and ensemble identity remain unaccepted.** The central potential pleasure is shaping productive ground and homes, then seeing actual people use the result. Real materials, meals and recoverable moves justify retaining that substrate. A viable unchanged village and voluntary finishing are deliberate peaceful choices. They leave the burden of motivation on visible transformation, which repeated roofs and small raised-bed fields do not yet satisfy.

The strongest case against the hybrid is that its broad management vocabulary promises needs and advancement while the opening already solves livelihood. More diagnostic controls explain that contradiction rather than resolve it. Passing tests and previous effort are not reasons to expand the catalogue or restore archived service certificates.

Choose a **productive-place comparison using existing systems**, with a more restrained interaction hierarchy. Make continuous cultivated ground, a modest working river landing and an oven workyard recognizable as different uses of land. Existing dock/boat and grain/bakery chains provide enough material differences; no additional resource, need or mandatory building. Keep all buildings accessible and the unchanged opening valid. Develop existing large forms before another roof-detail/shore-notch pass.

The alternative is a pure expressive composition game with management entirely secondary. A constrained logistics game is another option, but adding a hunger crisis merely to force choices would compromise the peaceful premise. Test the productive-place hybrid once more through visible construction and ordinary use; reject further economy expansion if the alternatives only change stock counters or need route statistics to justify themselves.
