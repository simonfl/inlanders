Reuse court/shared work/physical food collection, allow free multi-building arrangement, and retain optional welcoming. Compare with the current single-building trial. The next outcome must address recognizable activity and occlusion while testing whether freer composition supplies pleasure. Do not call lower travel time, reliable food or attractive roofs sufficient acceptance.

## Strongest objection and alternative

We may be optimizing an invisible logistics problem while removing movement that would reward watching. The court's 47.4 measured meal-walking resident-seconds are not inherently more entertaining than the control's 142.5. Actual central-pantry claims and overflow mean a garden move need not produce a garden-to-home journey. The reader exposes the behavior honestly; that does not make the behavior compelling. The metric excludes other production/hauling travel and must not be used as a whole-economy efficiency score.

The trial's one-building limit also interrupts a plausible intention: move several homes and reshape their shared ground before judging the whole. It was a bounded experiment, not an established ideal player rule. Free arrangement is a substantial alternative to managing a constrained relocation trial. It could fail because arranging feels arbitrary or because removing constraints removes purpose. Compare it rather than automatically adopting endless decoration.

Ask an observer to choose a spatial intention in an inhabited sixteen-resident scene: open a gathering place, make a lane, or form a garden neighborhood. They should recognize work, meal collection and social life with the diagnostic card closed. Fail if the result is mainly different roofs around an unreadable crowd, a prescribed goal is still needed, the card must remain open, or free arrangement removes the desire to observe/continue. If so, redesign the activity distribution before more levels or building art. Do not manufacture longer routes, hunger or quota requirements.

## Whole-game retention and cuts

- Retain shared work, physical collection/construction, homes and optional welcoming provisionally because they can connect arrangement to recognizable life. Functional tests do not prove that promise fulfilled.
- Keep eating, home return and communal activity as observable routines. Freeze new needs arithmetic until those routines read clearly in the world.
- Eighteen buildings remain available ingredients, not progression obligations. Geographically constrained food choices, processing and material investment can create differences; orchard, comfort and three civic scales still need experiential justification beyond prices/capacity.
- Preserve useful river, lake, quarry and woodland geography. Do not preserve ten levels of recipes/assessments simply because implemented. Freeze further campaign expansion.
- Unify Creative with the selected village rules. The historical Creative inspector still teaches manual roles; free construction should retain the same physical daily life with explicit forgiving rules.
- Retire unsuccessful player-facing experiment entries when their comparison is decided. Keep development fixtures and useful maps; do not expose our accumulated experiment history as the product.
- Keep the existing audio system as material to audition. Spatial cues, acceleration throttling, music miniatures and quiet intervals are sensible provisions, not evidence of a pleasant mix.

The lead accepts the review's direction. Its main disagreement with a narrow efficiency reading is decisive: shorter measured meal routes do not establish a better game. No additional balancing target or forced food journey is introduced to rescue that claim.
