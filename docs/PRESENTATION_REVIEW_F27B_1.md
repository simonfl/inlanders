## Whole-game verdict

**Partially convincing foundation, unconvincing present experience. Do not accept the candidate as F27b.** Physical cargo, recognizable inhabitants, entrances and a real welcome provide something worth developing. The scene still reads as objects on a board wrapped in management information. The candidate changes the board's finish more than the experience of inhabiting a place. Economy failures also remain unresolved; presentation cannot establish that the existing logistical complexity deserves to survive.

The independent reviewer and primary agreed on the strongest failures. Pale continuous bevels emphasize the simulation boundary. Straight narrow water, repeated roof rows and uninterrupted lawn dominate. Lower camera and longer shadows increase overlap around the gathering. Adding density or ornaments would not create a hierarchy of places. The completed Goals view repeats the payoff in several paragraphs before a visitor transaction; labels crowd the gathering.

## Retain, cut, replace

Retain human/building scale, entrances and physical cargo for their connection to visible action. Retain the clustered tree-crown vocabulary for variation, and the same-save comparison seam for honest iteration. Existing simple meshes and roof colors do not yet justify an expensive asset migration.

Cut the uniform pale bevel and global roof/camera exaggeration as the proposed direction. Stop further ornament passes before addressing terrain and outdoor space. Replace persistent communal labels and repeated completion prose with contextual identification and a concise milestone, keeping details available deliberately.

**Chosen next treatment: an inhabited river valley.** Add modest noninteractive landscape context beyond the buildable area and an irregular low riverbank profile. Preserve navigability and make usable boundaries explicit during placement. Connect the visual ground around house entrances, bridge arrival and communal activity; differentiate workplace/domestic/shared space without claiming cosmetic wear grants path speed. Use vegetation groups outside work routes and keep the active foreground open. Start with one home, one workplace and a communal venue, not all eighteen building models.

The reviewer considered a deliberate crafted tabletop a coherent alternative, but favored the valley given the user's complaint about flat appearance. The rejected study sat between those directions. Primary agrees; this is justified retention of a few ingredients, not acceptance of the candidate.
