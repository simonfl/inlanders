## Verdict and decision

All five verdicts are **partially convincing**. The central pleasure is choosing useful ground, seeing actual work and meals, arranging a place around that life, and watching it. The public forest–homes–cultivation–river ensemble supports that promise better than the archived dense board. Construction recovery, route-sensitive work and clearer observation now serve a chosen intention more coherently.

The strongest objection is that the game may be getting exceptionally good at serving an intention the player does not have. Establishment supplies a practical first decision. Once food is dependable, wanting another alteration or enjoying a quiet visit must carry the experience. Correctness, population, feature counts and scripted recoveries do not establish that desire. A short satisfied visit is valid; indifference after understanding is a structural failure, not an invitation to add another need or counter.

**Retain the existing public experience provisionally; stop210.** Freeze automatic catalogue, inspector and prop expansion. Compare management-led establishment with dependable-provision tending using existing starts and Normal/Relaxed modes before selecting another implementation batch. No211 is authorized. No inheritance, seasons, compulsory diet targets or save migrations.

## Independent findings and disagreements

- **Design:** retain physical consequence and optional stopping. Grain processing, storage, larger homes and civic venues have mechanically different costs/capacities, but their desirability is not established. Keep them available without requiring every chain. The dense finale is a tested older economy, not a desirable public end state.
- **UX:** everyday cards and named return reduce real hierarchy/navigation costs. A concrete remaining inconsistency exists in public square → Change this place → Details → Details & policies: its manual promises bread and supper in Goals although public founding hides supper. This was source-supported during review, not independently reproduced by the reviewer. The broader risk is understanding village life primarily through panels. Its substantial alternative is one direct-ground household/work-area alteration followed by quiet observation, reusing existing reversible rules.
- **Playtest:** recovery tests supply the desire for a bakery themselves. The new controls work in a hybrid native/model test; that is not an uncoached Normal session. The missing evidence is whether a player forms an intention, notices actual benefit, wants another change, or stops contentedly. Archived assessment windows cannot answer that question.
- **Development lead:** no definite new gameplay blocker found. Retain measured local rendering improvement and existing reliability tooling. Many shared Game/World partials and archived branches increase coupling; create local boundaries only when a concrete comparison needs them. An ECS rewrite, generalized cache or new experiment framework is unjustified.
- **Visual/audio:** retain readable low-poly forms, restrained materials, modest homes and quiet Watch. Reconsider the complete frontage: shallow productive strip, geometric bank, broad lawn and repeated woodland still weaken place identity. Dense archive/menu art has a brighter toy-diorama grammar than public river play. A larger card can cover the destination it explains. Audio source has spatial cues, bounded voices and musical pauses; pleasant sound, contact synchronization and accelerated-speed comfort remain unauditioned.

Agreement concerns the missing motivation evidence and value of physical consequences. Visual review favors a deeper farmstead/bank composition comparison; UX favors a more direct ground interaction; design/playtest favor comparing existing establish/tend first. **Choose the existing comparison first**, because new geometry or controls would confound the central question. Keep the two substantial alternatives conditional, not reduced to a safe queue of props/tooltips. The square-help correction addresses a specific contradiction separately; it does not resolve the structural verdict.

## Whole-game disposition

Public Normal retains material investment, meals and recoverable shortages; Relaxed retains work/meals while making construction free and removing hunger penalties. Legacy Creative disables meals; CreativeCourt retains them. Do not transfer evidence between these contracts without stating the difference.

Keep shared labor, actual stored materials/food, route-dependent choices, woodland care, reversible placement and ordinary rest/breaks. Existing needs are enough to test village life. Geography gives fishing, cultivation, gathering and crossing distinct roles. Hall/seating/square capacity and visit timing, processing steps and furnishing are hypotheses for voluntary choice, not progression obligations. Preserve existing catalogue access; the redundant carpenter dependency remains absent from public browsing because shared workers improve homes directly.

Keep the campaign archived. Its prescribed production chains, population growth, recent-service percentages, repeated assessments and bread supper are a different contract. Reuse spatial situations and recovery cases if useful; do not restore compliance as the public onboarding solution.

Retain the public ensemble provisionally, rather than preserving all existing art by inertia. A future matched deeper frontage should visibly connect water, home, yard, productive strips and retained woodland at the same inventory. Reject it if richness hides entrances or adds unhelpful travel without improving understood arrangement. A future compact observation/camera experiment must improve seeing destinations and returning without increasing search. A renderer replacement or catalogue-wide asset overhaul is unsupported.
