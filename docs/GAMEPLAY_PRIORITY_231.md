## Chosen direction

Gameplay first: meaningful objectives, resource/work tradeoffs, multiple viable plans and recoverable mistakes. Functional placement remains central where it changes access, transport or production. Automatic labor, reserve policies, woodland renewal, building groups, actual meals/materials and forgiving planning stay. Existing optional cosmetic controls need no deletion project.

Freeze expansion of player-selected decorations, roof/plaster variants, yard styling/furnishing, ornamental terrain and generalized group-composition tools. Do not create decoration requirements or rewards. Preserve New France identity and existing visuals. Authored/automatic detail can support the place without becoming player chores; visual work now needs a specific readability benefit or gameplay purpose.

Next conditional experiment: restore disconnected established food production versus establish accessible replacement supply, using current systems. Require two understandable approaches, a recoverable mistake and a reason to reconsider after consequences appear. Observe whether the player can explain why they chose, what they gave up and who benefited. If it stays two homes plus food then waiting, redesign or reduce the scale; do not add compulsory needs, quotas or chapters to conceal it.
