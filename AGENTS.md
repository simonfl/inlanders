# Inlanders working instructions

- Follow [the development process](docs/DEVELOPMENT_PROCESS.md). It supersedes checkpoint batches and the previous every-ten review schedule under the user's November 8 process reset.
- Keep one active experience hypothesis and bounded playable experiment in docs/NEXT_CHUNKS.md. Update docs/ROADMAP.md when evidence changes direction, and docs/CHECKPOINTS.md when a playable outcome is delivered. Count outcomes once. Documentation, tooling and commits alone do not advance the count.
- Use one independent read-only runtime evaluator for each playable experiment. Additional specialist agents require a named unresolved question. These evaluator and specialist subagents are authorized.
- At a substantial direction decision, promotion into production or substantial integrated release, run independent read-only game designer, UX/onboarding, playtest, development lead and visual/audio reviewers on a fixed commit/build. These subagents are authorized. Review the entire game, including campaign, economy/buildings/needs, public and Creative modes, controls, presentation/audio and reliability. State unobserved areas.
- Follow docs/DESIGN_REVIEW_MANDATE.md and docs/ITERATION_TOOLING_REVIEW.md subject to the new process. Challenge existing systems and architecture, consolidate disagreements, and choose keep, revise or drop. Existing implementation and test counts do not establish design value.
- When direction is questioned, assess it before routine implementation. Reuse the independent review of an unchanged build and seek the missing evidence instead of repeatedly convening the same review.
- Human play and preference inform experience acceptance. Keep that evidence distinct from scripted simulations, actual agent UI interaction, source inspection and audiovisual observation. Hold expansion when the active design question lacks evidence.
- Save backward compatibility is not required. Personal saves are disposable. Keep current-format saves correct and do not delete unrelated data.
